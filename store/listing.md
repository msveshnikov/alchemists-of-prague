## en-US (default)

Title (≤30): Alchemists of Prague

Short (≤80): Dark-fantasy action RPG in medieval Prague: 20 quests, swords and walking dead

Full:
Prague, Anno Domini 1357. The alchemists of the Black Sun have raised the dead. By day the market trades; by night skeletons climb out of the cellars and clay golems walk the Stone Bridge. You are Václav, a knight of Vyšehrad.

⚔️ ONE OPEN MEDIEVAL CITY
Walk without loading screens through four districts: Old Town Square with the Týn church and the astronomical clock, the Stone Bridge over the Vltava, Malá Strana below the castle and the fortress of Vyšehrad. Day turns to night, the market closes, lanterns light up and the dead come out.

📜 20 QUESTS, GOTHIC STYLE
A main story about the Brotherhood of the Black Sun and its Magister, and twelve side quests: carry goods between traders, find a child's lost toy, clear a haunted house, duel a cocky squire, deliver a letter to the castle. Talk to the people of Prague in classic dialogue windows.

🧭 ALWAYS KNOW WHERE TO GO
A gold ! over a head means work, a ? means someone is waiting for you. The objective, its progress and the distance are always on screen, a marker on the compass points the way, the map and the quest journal show the rest.

🗡️ SWORDS, AXES, GREATSWORDS
Buy a longer bastard sword, a war axe that splits golem clay or an executioner's greatsword; have the smith hone your blade. Block with your shield, roll away, chain strikes.

🪟 A PRAGUE TRADITION
Wound an alchemist, seize him and throw him out of the window. Or off the bridge into the Vltava. Or off the walls of Vyšehrad.

• Experience and levels, healing draughts, armour and rings
• Touch controls made for phones
• Works offline, no ads, no in-app purchases
• Text in English

Release notes (1.3.1): 20 quests with dialogue, a quest journal and compass markers; five weapons; experience and levels; faster graphics on phones.

Прага, 1357 год от Рождества Христова. Алхимики Чёрного Солнца подняли мёртвых. Днём на площади торгуют, а ночью из подвалов лезут скелеты и по Каменному мосту бродят глиняные големы. Ты — Вацлав, рыцарь из Вышеграда.

⚔️ ОДИН ОТКРЫТЫЙ СРЕДНЕВЕКОВЫЙ ГОРОД

📜 20 КВЕСТОВ В ДУХЕ «ГОТИКИ»
Основная история о братстве Чёрного Солнца и его Магистре и двенадцать побочных заданий: отнести товар торговцу, найти игрушку малыша, очистить дом с привидениями, проучить заносчивого оруженосца на дуэли, доставить письмо в замок. Разговоры — в классических окнах диалога.

🧭 ВСЕГДА ЯСНО, КУДА ИДТИ
